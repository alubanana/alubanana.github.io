An interactive research site for tracking technical progress, papers and companies. Records retain their original sources, event dates and reading scope. Changes to judgments are saved as versions for later comparison.

## Explore the radar

The public sample covers **5 themes, 10 technology directions and 18 companies**, with **44 events and 31 research entries**, as of **November 1, 2026**. The interface includes a theme map, scenario assumptions, risks, a relationship graph, historical checks, companies, milestones, papers and research criteria.

The sample is published as a static snapshot. An incremental check date records a completed review; opening the page does not update its evidence.

## Evidence and judgments

Company announcements, paper results and research judgments are recorded separately. Experiments retain the task, sample and comparison conditions. The reading scope identifies full text or abstract.

Scenario calculations use explicit human assumptions and evidence scores. Definitions, counterevidence and sensitivity ranges accompany the output. Previous inputs remain available when comparing versions.

## Implementation

Python checks fields, dates and references, then builds a static page. JavaScript provides filtering, company details, watchlists, JSON export and scenario calculations. I define the research questions and information structure; AI helps implement the code.
